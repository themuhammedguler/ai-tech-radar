### 2. [Tokenisation is not in APX](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34938v1)
- 👥 **Yazarlar:** Violeta Kastreva, Philip Whittington, Dennis Komm vb.
- 📅 **Yayın Tarihi:** `2026-11-25`
- 🏷️ **Kategori:** `cs.CL`
- 🔗 **Bağlantı:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34938v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34938v1)
- 📝 **Özet:** *In this paper, we prove that, unless P=NP, no polynomial-time algorithm can find a tokeniser whose objective is within any constant factor of the optimum, meaning that tokenisation is not in the APX complexity class. We prove this for direct and bottom-up tokenisation, under both a compression or unigram log-likelihood objectives, and for binary or larger alphabets.*
