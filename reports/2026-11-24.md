### 3. [TowerBridge: Bridging the Dual-Encoder Modality Gap via Weight-Space Programming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31233v1)
- 👥 **Yazarlar:** Hani Alomari, Md Atabuzzaman, Chris Thomas
- 📅 **Yayın Tarihi:** `2026-11-23`
