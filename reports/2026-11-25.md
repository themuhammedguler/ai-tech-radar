### 1. [OmniAct: One Policy, One Million Embodiments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33158v1)
- 👥 **Yazarlar:** Xiang Fan, Yuheng Wang, Jiayuan Yan vb.
- 📅 **Yayın Tarihi:** `2026-11-24`
- 🏷️ **Kategori:** `cs.RO`
- 🔗 **Bağlantı:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33158v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33158v1)
- 📝 **Özet:** *A single policy that controls diverse robots must not only transfer task knowledge across embodiments, but also adapt its behavior to each robot's capabilities. Existing approaches facilitate transfer through shared action representations, but these representations do not necessarily capture how differences in robot morphology should shape manipulation strategies.*

### 2. [DiaCap: Jointly Learning Who Speaks When for Audiovisual Dialogue Captioning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33153v1)
- 👥 **Yazarlar:** Binfei Ji, Zhiwen Cao, Anqi Yang vb.
- 📅 **Yayın Tarihi:** `2026-11-24`
- 🏷️ **Kategori:** `cs.CV`
- 🔗 **Bağlantı:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33153v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33153v1)
- 📝 **Özet:** *We present DiaCap, an audiovisual LLM for comprehensive dialogue captioning, which produces the transcript, timestamps, and a visual description of the speaker for every utterance in a video. Considering that utterance timing and audiovisual diarization are tightly coupled, DiaCap unifies the tasks in a single model, trained in two complementary stages.*

### 3. [BioLoom: Scaling Biomedical Vision-Language Models by Learning from the Structure of Scientific Articles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33151v1)
- 👥 **Yazarlar:** Yuwei Yang, Xiaoxiao Sun, Alejandro Lozano vb.

- 📝 **Özet:** *The development of biomedical vision-language models (VLMs) with contrastive image-text pretraining has been driven by scaling to increasingly large and diverse image-text datasets. Beyond scaling, further progress can come from learning richer representations from existing data.*

### 4. [CoT-Proxy: Cross-Format Transfer for Distilling Long-Horizon Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33148v1)
- 👥 **Yazarlar:** Dawid J. Kopiczko, Tijmen Blankevoort, Yuki M. Asano
- 📅 **Yayın Tarihi:** `2026-11-24`
- 🏷️ **Kategori:** `cs.CL`
- 🔗 **Bağlantı:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33148v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33148v1)
- 📝 **Özet:** *Reasoning models become long-horizon agents through reinforcement learning or, more cheaply, through fine-tuning on a stronger teacher's trajectories, which contain the teacher's chain-of-thought as well as its actions. The standard fine-tuning approach imitates both, yet the strongest teachers are closed models whose chain-of-thought is hidden behind an API.*

### 5. [iWAM: Efficient Action Prediction from World Models without Explicit Future Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33141v1)
- 👥 **Yazarlar:** Vansh Kapoor, Max Sobol Mark, Sreyas Venkataraman vb.
- 📅 **Yayın Tarihi:** `2026-11-24`
- 🏷️ **Kategori:** `cs.RO`
- 🔗 **Bağlantı:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33141v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33141v1)
- 📝 **Özet:** *World-action models (WAMs) build policies on pretrained video-generation models, on the premise that predicting how a scene evolves helps determine how to act. However, generating future scenes is expensive for real-time control. This motivates efficient proxies for future imagination, but how to train such proxies without sacrificing action-prediction quality remains unclear.*
