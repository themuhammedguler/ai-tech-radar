- 🏷️ **Kategori:** `cs.AI`
- 🔗 **Bağlantı:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08483v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08483v1)
- 📝 **Özet:** *LLM-guided evolutionary search uses AI agents to improve programs for mathematical and computational problems by iteratively refining solutions and combining high-scoring methods. ### 3. [Scientist World: Grow a Research World for AI-Scientists](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08478v1)
- 👥 **Yazarlar:** Taiming Lu, Sachin Konan, Zhuang Liu
- 🏷️ **Kategori:** `cs.AI`
- 🔗 **Bağlantı:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08478v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08478v1)
- 📝 **Özet:** *AI-Scientists arrived, but a world for them to grow in did not. Human scientists take theirs for granted $-$ centuries of open problems and shared ideas, waiting in the scientific literature. To AI-Scientists, that world is readable but not runnable, so we build Scientist World for them: every research paper becomes an executable environment.*

### 5. [Same Behavior, Different Mechanism: What Finite Experiments Can Certify About Integrated Information](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08469v1)

- 🏷️ **Kategori:** `cs.LG`
