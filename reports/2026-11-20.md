- 👥 **Yazarlar:** Weihao Cheng
- 📅 **Yayın Tarihi:** `2026-11-19`
- 🏷️ **Kategori:** `cs.AI`

We propose PlayCraft, an automated framework that uses a vision-based agent to play games and draws on its detailed play experience to revise them for mastery.*

- 👥 **Yazarlar:** Yuxin Jiang, Wenqiang Sun, Jiaqi Li vb.
- 📅 **Yayın Tarihi:** `2026-11-19`

We ask whether a pretrained video generator can instead provide the prior for both inverse and forward dynamics.*
