### 1. [User Embeddings are Superpositions of Interpretable Behavioral Modes](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08487v1)

- 🏷️ **Kategori:** `cs.LG`
- 🔗 **Bağlantı:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08487v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08487v1)
- 📝 **Özet:** *User embeddings are central components of modern recommendation, personalization, and behavioral modeling systems. Though these embeddings can compactly encode rich behavioral information, they are inherently dense and opaque, limiting interpretability.*

### 2. [SwarmEvolve: Latent Agent Swarms for Evolutionary Discovery and Optimization](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08483v1)
- 👥 **Yazarlar:** Qile Jiang, George Em Karniadakis
- 🏷️ **Kategori:** `cs.AI`
- 🔗 **Bağlantı:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08483v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08483v1)
- 📝 **Özet:** *LLM-guided evolutionary search uses AI agents to improve programs for mathematical and computational problems by iteratively refining solutions and combining high-scoring methods. However, the process is often slow because the LLM generates a long textual chain of thought for each round and must repeat this generation many times to maintain population diversity.*

### 3. [Scientist World: Grow a Research World for AI-Scientists](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08478v1)
- 👥 **Yazarlar:** Taiming Lu, Sachin Konan, Zhuang Liu
- 🏷️ **Kategori:** `cs.AI`
- 🔗 **Bağlantı:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08478v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08478v1)
- 📝 **Özet:** *AI-Scientists arrived, but a world for them to grow in did not. Human scientists take theirs for granted $-$ centuries of open problems and shared ideas, waiting in the scientific literature. To AI-Scientists, that world is readable but not runnable, so we build Scientist World for them: every research paper becomes an executable environment.*

### 4. [XStack: A Benchmark for ML Repo Migration](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08472v1)
- 👥 **Yazarlar:** Linrong Cai, Taiming Lu, Haozhe Chen vb.
- 🏷️ **Kategori:** `cs.LG`
- 🔗 **Bağlantı:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08472v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08472v1)
- 📝 **Özet:** *ML code should run where researchers choose, not where it was first written. Today, machine learning code mainly lives in two ecosystems, PyTorch on NVIDIA GPUs and JAX on Google TPUs. Migrating repositories between them means building and testing across frameworks and hardware.*

### 5. [Same Behavior, Different Mechanism: What Finite Experiments Can Certify About Integrated Information](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08469v1)
- 👥 **Yazarlar:** Linqian Wu, Diaoxuan He
- 🏷️ **Kategori:** `cs.LG`
- 🔗 **Bağlantı:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08469v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08469v1)
- 📝 **Özet:** *Interpretability, AI auditing and assessments of machine consciousness increasingly rest on mechanism-level quantities: functionals of a system's causal transition law that describe how it computes, not what it outputs. Experiments see that law only through finite, partial and noisy interventions.*
