- 👥 **Yazarlar:** Weihao Cheng
- 📅 **Yayın Tarihi:** `2026-11-19`
- 🏷️ **Kategori:** `cs.AI`
- 🔗 **Bağlantı:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26624v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26624v1)
- 📝 **Özet:** *Recent AI agents can generate playable games, but these games are not necessarily engaging. Mastery is a key source of engagement, yet designing for it requires extensive human play and tuning. We propose PlayCraft, an automated framework that uses a vision-based agent to play games and draws on its detailed play experience to revise them for mastery.*

### 4. [One Prior, Two Dynamics: Repurposing Video Diffusion Transformers for Self-Supervised Latent Action Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26617v1)
- 👥 **Yazarlar:** Yuxin Jiang, Wenqiang Sun, Jiaqi Li vb.
- 📅 **Yayın Tarihi:** `2026-11-19`
- 🏷️ **Kategori:** `cs.CV`
- 🔗 **Bağlantı:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26617v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26617v1)
- 📝 **Özet:** *Videos reveal how the world changes, but rarely the actions that cause those changes. Latent action learning recovers such supervision from visual transitions, yet often learns the underlying dynamics largely from scratch. We ask whether a pretrained video generator can instead provide the prior for both inverse and forward dynamics.*
